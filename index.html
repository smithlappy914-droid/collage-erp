COMPETITIVE EXCELLENCE CELL

JEE Advanced, NEET & Olympiad Foundation Wing

At St. Joseph's School, Basti, we prepare students beyond regular board examinations. Our structured competitive curriculum builds analytical depth, speed, and problem-solving mastery from Class 6 to Class 12.

Classes 6 to 8

Junior Olympiad & Logic Foundation

Focuses on Mathematical Olympiads (IMO), National Science Olympiad (NSO), mental aptitude, Vedic mathematics, and foundational coding concepts to spark early analytical thinking.

Classes 9 & 10

Pre-Foundation (NTSE & IJSO)

Accelerated training in vector physics, mole concept, advanced algebra, and trigonometry aligned with NTSE, KVPY, and Junior Science Olympiad standards alongside ICSE/CBSE board prep.

Classes 11 & 12

JEE Main, JEE Advanced & NEET Target

Rigorous Concept-Application modules, weekly CBT (Computer Based Test) mock exams, doubt-remediation clinics, and rank-booster test series tracked directly on the monthly parent report card.
